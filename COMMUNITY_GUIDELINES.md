# ChatFort — Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** March 6, 2026
**Last Updated:** October 19, 2026

ChatFort is a secure messaging app built for activists, organizers, journalists, and communities who need private communication. These guidelines exist to ensure the platform remains safe and respectful for everyone.

---

## Our Principles

1. **Privacy is a right, not a privilege.** Everyone deserves secure communication free from surveillance.
2. **Security serves people.** Our tools exist to protect those facing real threats.
3. **Respect is non-negotiable.** Dignity for all users regardless of identity, background, or beliefs.
4. **Community protects community.** Users help keep the platform safe through reporting and moderation tools.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Treat all users with respect and dignity.
- Protect the privacy of others — do not share private conversations or personal information without consent.
- Use group features responsibly — respect group rules set by administrators.
- Report abusive content or behavior using the in-app tools: in groups, and in the crowd mesh.
- Use security features (emergency wipe, app lock) for their intended protective purpose.

---

## Prohibited Content and Behavior

The following are strictly prohibited on ChatFort:

### Exploitation and Abuse
- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, or any other sexual abuse or exploitation of children (see [Child Safety Standards](#child-safety-standards)).
- Human trafficking, slavery, or exploitation of persons.
- Non-consensual intimate imagery ("revenge porn").

### Violence and Terrorism
- Coordinating, planning, or promoting acts of terrorism or violent extremism.
- Credible threats of violence against individuals or groups.
- Content that incites violence based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or national origin.

### Harassment
- Stalking, bullying, or sustained intimidation of other users.
- Doxxing — sharing someone's private information (address, phone number, workplace) without consent.
- Targeted harassment campaigns against individuals or groups.

### Fraud and Deception
- Impersonating another person or organization with intent to deceive.
- Phishing or social engineering attacks against other users.
- Distributing malware, spyware, or other harmful software.

### Spam
- Sending unsolicited bulk messages.
- Creating groups solely for advertising or commercial spam.
- Automated or bot-driven messaging without user consent.

## Child Safety Standards

ChatFort, published by Your Healing Artist Limited, has **zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE)**. These standards apply everywhere in ChatFort: private chats, groups, calls, files, Nearby rooms and the crowd mesh.

### Age

ChatFort is only for people aged **18 and over**. On Google Play, ChatFort is restricted to adults.

### What is prohibited

You must not use ChatFort to:

- Create, share, request, store or link to child sexual abuse material (CSAM), including drawn, edited or AI-generated material.
- Groom a child, sexualise a child, or contact a child for sexual purposes, online or in person.
- Sexually extort a child ("sextortion"), or threaten or coerce a child in any way.
- Traffic, sell or exploit children.
- Advertise, promote, organise or help with any of the above.

### How to report

- **Crowd mesh:** long-press the message, then tap **Report**. This opens an email to the ChatFort team with the message, the sender's nickname and the time. Tap **Report and block** to block the sender at the same time.
- **Groups:** long-press the message and report it to the group's administrators, and email us as well.
- **Any chat:** email us from **Settings › Send us email**, or write to [chatfort@yourhealingartist.com](mailto:chatfort@yourhealingartist.com) with the subject "Child safety". Use **Block & Delete** to cut off a contact.
- **If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local police or emergency services first.**

### What we do

- We review every child safety report first, ahead of other reports.
- Messages are end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot see them unless someone reports them to us. When we learn of CSAM, for example from a report, we act on it.
- Where the material, or a link to it, is on servers we operate, we remove it or block access to it.
- We report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant authority, such as the UK National Crime Agency.
- We cooperate with law enforcement, within what we hold. ChatFort has no accounts, phone numbers or message content to hand over.
- The crowd mesh has no servers. Content there stays on the phones that received it, and we cannot delete it from them. Block the sender, and report them to us and to the police.

### Child safety contact

Our child safety point of contact can be reached at [chatfort@yourhealingartist.com](mailto:chatfort@yourhealingartist.com) (subject "Child safety"). They can answer questions about these standards and how we enforce them.

## Moderation and Enforcement

ChatFort uses end-to-end encryption. This means:

- **We cannot read your messages.** Content moderation relies on user-driven tools.
- **We do not monitor conversations.** There is no scanning on any server. The crowd mesh word filter (below) runs only on your phone and sends nothing to us.
- **We do not have access to your data.** Enforcement depends on community participation.

**We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users.**

### Tools Available to Users

| Tool | Scope | What it does |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **Report message** | Groups | Flags a specific message for review by group administrators. |
| **Block member** | Groups | Group administrators can block a member from posting in the group. |
| **Remove member** | Groups | Group administrators can remove a member from the group entirely. |
| **Leave group** | Groups | Removes you from any group instantly. |
| **Group permissions** | Groups | Administrators can control who can send messages, add members, or share content. |
| **Block & Delete** | 1:1 chats | Permanently blocks a contact, deletes all messages, and prevents them from messaging you again. They are not notified. |
| **Clear chat** | Any chat | Permanently removes message history from your device. |
| **Report message** | Crowd mesh | Long-press a message from someone else: opens an email to the ChatFort team with the message, the sender's nickname and the time. |
| **Block** | Crowd mesh | Long-press a message or a person, or use Block in a private chat. Removes their messages from your phone, hides them from People and stops them messaging you privately. Messages from anyone using the same nickname are hidden too. Unblock in People › Blocked. |
| **Word filter** | Crowd mesh | The Everyone channel won't send a message with offensive words (English, Roman Urdu, Urdu, Hindi). Received messages with such words are hidden until you tap Show. |
| **Community rules** | Crowd mesh | You agree to the crowd mesh rules before you first start it. Read them again in People › Safety. |

### What Happens When You Report

**In groups:**

- Reports are visible to **group administrators only** (not to ChatFort developers or server operators).
- Group administrators can take action including blocking or removing the reported member from the group.
- ChatFort does not receive, store, or process report data on any server.
- For unwanted 1:1 contacts, use Block & Delete.

**In the crowd mesh:**

- A report is an email to the ChatFort team at [chatfort@yourhealingartist.com](mailto:chatfort@yourhealingartist.com), sent from your own email app. It contains the message (for a photo or voice note, its caption), the sender's nickname, the first part of their mesh ID, the time, and the app version. We also see your email address. The team name is not included.
- With no email app, you can copy the report and send it when you're online.
- We review every report and respond promptly. The crowd mesh has no servers or accounts, so we cannot delete messages from other people's phones or ban a person from the mesh. Block the sender to stop seeing them at once.

## If You Experience Abuse

### In a group
1. **Report the message** using the in-app reporting feature (long-press the message).
2. **Leave the group** if the environment feels unsafe.
3. **Document evidence** — take screenshots before deleting if you may need them for authorities.
4. **Contact local authorities** if you face credible threats of violence or illegal activity.

### In a 1:1 conversation
1. **Block & Delete the contact** to permanently stop all messages from that person.
2. **Document evidence** — take screenshots before deleting if you may need them for authorities.
3. **Contact local authorities** if you face credible threats of violence or illegal activity.

### In the crowd mesh
1. **Report the message** (long-press it, then Report). Choose **Report and block** to block the sender at the same time.
2. **Block the person** (long-press their message or their name in People, or use Block in a private chat).
3. **Document evidence** before blocking: blocking removes their messages from your phone.
4. **Contact local authorities** if you face credible threats of violence or illegal activity.

If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services.

## For Group Administrators

As a group administrator, you have a responsibility to:

- Set clear expectations for your group.
- Respond to reports from group members.
- Remove members who violate these guidelines.
- Use group permissions to control who can send messages, add members, or share content.
